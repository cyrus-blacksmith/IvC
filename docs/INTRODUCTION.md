# Section Introduction: IvC Framework

Each evening near Rome, thousands of starlings gather in murmurations, weaving through the sky as a single, fluid entity. This paper proposes that this principle—a state where individual separation appears as an illusion and systemic connection becomes the functional reality—is a universal property of matter when it crosses a critical threshold of geometric and energetic convergence.

We define coherence operationally as a measurable dynamical regime in which multiple oscillatory degrees of freedom become phase-coupled across space and modality, cross-modal feedback renders this coordination self-reinforcing, and the resulting global order becomes robust to perturbation. 

For decades, the central question of biology has been: "Which molecule does X?" This research suggests a more fundamental question: "What coherent rhythm defines X?" By shifting our focus from the "parts" to the "phase-locking" of those parts, we can begin to understand how life maintains such extraordinary order amidst the chaos of its environment. This paper provides the mathematical and physical foundation for this shift, denoted as the **IvC framework**.
