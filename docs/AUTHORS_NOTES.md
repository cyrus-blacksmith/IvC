# Authors Note

### A Personal Note

This monograph represents the culmination of a year of work, inspired in large part by the foundational research of Michael Levin and colleagues on bioelectricity, pattern formation, and the plasticity of living systems. It is offered to the scientific community as a starting point, not an endpoint, an invitation to explore, critique, and build upon the ideas presented here.

If this framework proves to have value, I hope others will carry it forward far beyond what I could accomplish alone. It is offered as a seed, to be tested, transformed, and extended by those who find it useful. With that hope comes a responsibility: that this knowledge be stewarded thoughtfully, guided by ethical foresight, and guarded against applications that could cause harm, whether through autonomous systems, bio-hybrid technologies, or any other form it may take. The same principles that enable life to cohere can, if misapplied, enable new kinds of disruption. May those who build on this work do so with care.

I am also deeply grateful for the role of artificial intelligence in this process. As a non-scientist by training, I would not have been able to navigate the cross-disciplinary depth required to synthesize biology, physics, mathematics, and engineering without the assistance of AI tools that helped translate concepts, generate code, and refine ideas across fields. This work is a testament to what becomes possible when human curiosity and machine collaboration converge.

Readers seeking a quick overview may read the Plain Language Summary, the introductory and concluding chapters, and the section summaries throughout. For those wishing to engage with the full evidence, mathematical derivations, and case studies, the complete text provides comprehensive depth.

With gratitude and hope,

**Cyrus Blacksmith** February 26, 2026
