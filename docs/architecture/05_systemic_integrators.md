# 05. Systemic Integrators: Organismal Coordination (Section 2.4)

Systemic integrators are higher-order rhythms that stabilize the cellular hubs across the entire organism.

### 2.4.1 Cardiorespiratory Rhythms
The heart and lungs act as the primary mechanical and rhythmic "pumps" of the system. They provide the fundamental "carrier wave" that entrains lower-frequency biological processes.

### 2.4.2 The Neuro-Glymphatic Axis
This axis manages fluid flux and global electromagnetic coordination. The rhythmic "pulsing" of the glymphatic system during sleep is essential for maintaining the clarity of the biophotonic and bioelectric signaling channels.

### 2.4.3 Circadian & Ultradian Cycles
These are the master timing regulators. They define the temporal "windows" in which metabolic coherence and the Topological Shield are reinforced or repaired.
