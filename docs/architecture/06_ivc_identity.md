# 06. The IvC Identity & Logic (Sections 2.5–2.6)

The framework is mathematically summarized by the IvC Identity, which defines the transition from a collection of parts to a living system.

### 2.5 The Identity: $I = \Psi(kLFH)$
* **Ignition ($I$)**: The emergence of a self-sustaining coherent state.
* **Topological Shield ($\Psi$)**: The protected state that emerges once phase-locking occurs.
* **Substrate Impedance ($k$)**: The resistance to signal flow (minimized by ordered water).
* **Lattice Density ($L$)**: The connectivity of the cytoskeletal and ECM scaffolds.
* **Flux ($F$)**: The energetic drive provided by metabolic/mitochondrial activity.
* **Hub Mass ($H$)**: The total capacity of the six primary hubs to process and integrate signals.

### 2.6 The Spectral Gap & Bifurcation
Ignition is a non-linear event. As $F$ (Flux) increases and $k$ (Impedance) decreases, the system reaches a bifurcation point where the hubs phase-lock. This creates a "Spectral Gap," a state where the system's internal rhythms are isolated from external noise, enabling the biological "order" to persist against entropy.
