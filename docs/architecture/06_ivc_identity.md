### 2.6 The Identity: I = Psi(kLFH)
Ignition occurs when the internal parameters of the system reach a critical threshold. Lattice Density (L) is the connectivity and structural integrity of the cytoskeletal and extracellular scaffolds. Flux (F) is the energetic drive provided by metabolic and mitochondrial activity. Hub Mass (H) is the total integration capacity of the six primary hubs. When F increases and k decreases, the system's coupled oscillators reach a critical coupling strength.

### 2.7 The Spectral Gap and Bifurcation
Ignition is a non-linear phase transition. At the moment of Ignition, a Spectral Gap opens, isolating the system's internal resonant frequencies from external environmental noise. This gap constitutes the Topological Shield (Psi), a mathematically protected state that allows biological order to persist and self-repair against entropy. Predictions of the framework include cross-modal entrainment, where disruption of one modality produces measurable shifts in others, and substrate sensitivity, where altering the state of nanoconfined water shifts the bifurcation threshold for Ignition.

### 2.8 Closing Synthesis
The IvC framework is both defined and adaptable, grounded in the 4+6 core where four modalities converge through six hubs to generate coherence across scales. This architecture is resilient, allowing for the incorporation of new hubs or substrates as evidence grows. Ultimately, IvC positions resonance not as a metaphor, but as life's measurable and testable architecture.
